Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This page explains what Bathroom Excellence collects when you use our online appointment questionnaire, why we collect it, who it is shared with, and how you can opt out of messages from us.

What we collect

When you complete the appointment questionnaire, we collect:

  • Contact details — your name, phone number, email address and the address of the property.
  • Project details — the type of work you are considering, your timeline, whether you have received other quotes and their approximate value, who will be involved in the decision, and how you plan to pay.
  • Financing information — if you tell us you want to explore financing, we ask for a general credit range. We never ask for and never collect a Social Security number, date of birth, bank details or a full credit report through this form.
  • Technical information — the page you arrived from, any advertising campaign identifiers in the link you followed, and the date and time of your submission.

Why we collect it

  • To contact you and arrange your in-home consultation.
  • To send the right consultant, with the right information, to the right address.
  • To prepare an accurate written price for your project.
  • To send you messages about your enquiry, where you have agreed to receive them.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third-party lead buyers or marketing lists.

Who it is shared with

Your information is handled by the service providers that run our business systems — our customer records system, our phone and messaging platform, our scheduling automation, and our email provider. They process it on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

SMS messaging program

Consent is not a condition of purchase. You can book a consultation without agreeing to receive text messages. If you leave the consent box unticked, we simply will not text or send you marketing email — your booking is unaffected.

  • Program: appointment and project updates from Bathroom Excellence.
  • Message frequency varies depending on your enquiry and appointment.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • To stop: reply STOP to any text and you will receive no further messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or call us on (636) 795-4643.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Messages that confirm an appointment you have actively booked are transactional and may still be sent so that you have a written record of the time and date.

Email

If you agreed to receive email from us, you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any message, or by contacting us directly.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and customer records for as long as needed to provide the service, to honour warranty obligations, and to meet our legal and accounting requirements. Records of your messaging consent — including when it was given and the exact wording shown to you — are retained for as long as we may need to evidence it.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop text messages.
  • Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Contact us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it.
